# Place-name data

The searchable Ukraine city and town list in ukraine-cities.json is derived from the OpenStreetMap based ua-name-places.csv dataset maintained by gontsa: https://github.com/gontsa/ua-osm-names-of-places
The CSV was downloaded on 2026-10-14. This app retains records tagged place=city or place=town and the name, region, and rayon fields (1,241 records in this snapshot).
OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Names and administrative assignments in occupied territories may be outdated; users can type a missing or corrected city name manually.
